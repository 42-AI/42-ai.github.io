- Tristan Deborde, CEO de Gigax
- Elliot Andres, CEO de PhotoRoom
- Benoît MARTIN: CTO, Naaia - Artificial Intelligence Management System™ (AIMS)
- Eric WETZEL: Data & AI Business Expert, Databricks
